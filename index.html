Install help

Android may stop you twice. Here is why, and the fix.

HyDeck is a new app that you install from this website, so Android is careful with it. Both screens below are normal for apps installed from a website, and both take under a minute. The pictures are simple illustrations: the words differ a little between phone brands.

1 "Blocked by Play Protect" while installing

Google Play Protect warns about apps it has not seen on many phones. It does not mean HyDeck is broken: you can check the SHA-256 fingerprint in the download box against the file you got.

App blocked to protect your device
Learn moreOK
  1. Open the Play Store, tap your profile picture, then Play Protect.
  2. Tap the gear (top right) and switch off Scan apps with Play Protect.
  3. Download and install HyDeck again.
  4. Switch Play Protect back on. HyDeck keeps working.

If your phone shows More details → Install anyway, just use that instead.

2 "Access denied" on notification access

HyDeck needs notification access to read the song that is playing. Android 13 and newer lock that switch for apps installed outside the Play Store until you allow it yourself.

App info⋮
Allow restricted settings
  1. Press and hold the HyDeck icon, then tap App info (or Settings → Apps → HyDeck).
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu at the top right.
  3. Tap Allow restricted settings and approve it with your PIN or fingerprint.
  4. Open HyDeck again and switch on Notification access.

No ⋮ menu? Try to switch notification access on once first; the menu appears after Android blocks it.

Questions

Good to know.

Is what I see here the real app?

Yes. Every look on this page is a real screenshot or recording of the app running on a real Android phone. The only made-up things are the demo song and its artwork, so no real music is shown. The one extra is the "match the vibe" box, a simplified version of the idea.

Is it really free?

Yes. HyDeck and its three built-in looks are free. Extra theme packs, like MIDAS and GYM, are optional and paid, and they are locked until you enter an unlock code.

Which music apps work?

Anything that shows a now-playing card on your phone: Spotify, YouTube Music, YouTube, TikTok and more. One note: YouTube Music only keeps playing in the background with YouTube Music Premium. That is YouTube's rule, not HyDeck's, so without Premium the deck follows it only while the YouTube Music app stays open. You can also share a YouTube or TikTok link to HyDeck to watch it in the in-app player.

Can it hear my music to pick a look?

No, it can't hear audio. "Match the vibe" reads the song title, artist and genre tags, and guesses the mood from the words.

How do theme packs work without the internet?

Each pack unlocks with a code made only for your phone. It is checked on the phone itself, so it works offline.

I reinstalled and my pack is locked.

A reinstall makes a new device code. Send us the new one and we will make you a new unlock code.

Can I use it on a computer or an iPhone?

Not yet. HyDeck is for Android phones. This website works on any computer, phone or tablet so everyone can see what it is.
